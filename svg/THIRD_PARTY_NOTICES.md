# Banner icon sources

The Docker, AWS, Kubernetes, Terraform, Python, and Linux logos embedded in
`ngocthai.svg` are from [Devicon](https://github.com/devicons/devicon), pinned to
commit `6a37858047b0e8d9c9c0e68f4a071fc410652e89`.

Source files under `icons/`: `docker/docker-original.svg`,
`amazonwebservices/amazonwebservices-original-wordmark.svg`,
`kubernetes/kubernetes-original.svg`, `terraform/terraform-original.svg`,
`python/python-original.svg`, and `linux/linux-plain.svg`.
